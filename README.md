# Scaling Global Audit & Risk Platforms — Case Study (Deloitte)

> Owning 45+ enterprise applications for Deloitte's Audit and Risk practice: scaling DeloitteDex from a US-only tool to 29 countries, driving $20M in ARR growth, cutting platform vulnerability profiles 89%, and helping Deloitte earn AWS Partner of the Year (2020).

*Public summary of my work as Product Owner, Risk & Innovation at Deloitte & Touche LLP (2017–2020). No proprietary data or client information.*

## Scope
- **45+ enterprise applications** across Audit and Risk, including the **My Audit** core platform, a **28-app Enterprise Integrations suite**, and PCAOB audit applications
- Built out the **product management procedures and team** for the portfolio
- Global infrastructure build-outs (SaaS, PaaS, IaaS) for clients and member firms using a **security-by-design (DevSecOps)** approach

## What I built and scaled
| Initiative | What it did |
|---|---|
| **DeloitteDex** | Enterprise survey, real-time data collection and analytics platform — scaled from a US-only tool to a unified SaaS/PaaS across **29 countries**, including a startup market expansion into Asia-Pacific that grew revenue **10%** |
| **Flagship audit apps** | Global rollouts of **ICount** (mobile-first asset auditing), **IConfirm**, **Connect** and **Reveal** |
| **China Cloud** | SaaS/PaaS infrastructure rollout for China with Microsoft |
| **Marketplace & Risk Intelligence platform** | Audit Insights, Company & Market Intelligence, People Intelligence, Briefing Book and Pursuit |
| **Automation** | Salesforce apps and **Audit automation bots** |
| **Transformation roadmap** | Agile, Cloud, IoT/Edge and DevSecOps transformation for the portfolio |

## Security, privacy and compliance
- Led **GDPR, NIST RMF, ISO 27001, Third-Party Risk** and **Veracode DAST/SAST** programs
- Risk management, controls testing, penetration testing and threat-informed recommendations
- **Cut platform vulnerability profiles 89%**
- Created Deloitte's **application sunset standard** for retiring legacy apps safely

## Lessons
1. **Build once, localize everywhere.** A unified platform made 29-country scale possible without 29 versions.
2. **Security by design beats security by audit.** Building controls into the pipeline is what cut vulnerabilities 89%.
3. **Retiring apps is a product decision.** A clear sunset standard reduced risk and freed budget for new work.
4. **Automation earns trust in audit.** Bots and integrations only work when every output is traceable.
